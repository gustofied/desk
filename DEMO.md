# Desk demo

A one-minute tour through Catalog, Monitor, and Craft.

## 0:00 — Catalog

Open [Desk](https://desk.adamsioud.com) and switch between Overview, Hedge,
and Private. The Catalog is the set of market views kept on your desk. Drag a
view to move it.

## 0:15 — Monitor

Open H200. Move across the chart to inspect the series, change the range, and
open Desk API to see the matching CLI command or DataFusion SQL.

## 0:30 — Craft

Open Craft, choose Price history, then open Data. Add H100 and B200, change the
chart between Price, Change, and Spread, and adjust the range.

## 0:50 — Keep and share

Save the view into the active Catalog. Press `⌘G` and choose **Copy view link**
to share the exact composition.
