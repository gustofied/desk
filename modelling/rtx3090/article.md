# Modelling GPU rental prices

[Read the exemplar on adamsioud.com](https://www.adamsioud.com/exemplars/gpu-prices/).

The article studies whether RTX 3090 repricing is isolated or widespread, using a daily price index, a historical baseline and seller participation. Its three D3 chapters read the notebook’s exported daily values. The downloadable notebook and static figures retain the Matplotlib rendering.

- [Executed notebook](price_model.ipynb)
- `price_model.html`: local research report, generated by `run_price_model.py`.
- [Industry methodology notes](index_methodology_notes.md)
- [Pearl research notes](pearl_notes.md)
