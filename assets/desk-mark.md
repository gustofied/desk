# Desk mark

`desk-mark.png` is extracted directly from Adam's supplied `IMG_1696.HEIC`.
It retains the photographed ink geometry; it is not generated artwork, a font,
or a vector reconstruction. Paper, pencil grid, disconnected photo dust, and
camera metadata were removed. The original photo was not changed.

The login displays this complete PNG at 128px wide. A clipped copy reveals
the original ink from left to right over a dimmed copy, then rests at full
opacity. No part of the logo is separated, translated, or redrawn.

The seven-second loop runs whether Desk is opened by click or shortcut.
Shortcut opening and keyboard login remain instant. Reduced motion shows
the complete mark without animation; closing or hiding the page cancels it.
